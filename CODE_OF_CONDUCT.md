# Code of conduct

Tessera follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep
criticism about the work, not the person. Harassment of any kind is not
tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/MrNechev/tessera/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.
